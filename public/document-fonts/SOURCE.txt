Inter-Regular.ttf and Inter-Bold.ttf are static instances (optical size 14,
weights 400 and 700) of the pinned Google Fonts Inter variable TrueType file,
generated with FontTools varLib.instancer. The Inter OFL license is in inter/OFL.txt.
